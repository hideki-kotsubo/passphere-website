2. The Service

Passphere is a zero-knowledge, offline-first password manager. It lets you store passwords, usernames, URLs and notes in one or more encrypted vaults on your device.

  • No account. Passphere does not require you to sign up, and we do not run any server that stores your vaults.
  • Local encryption. Your vault is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your master password.
  • Optional sync. You can sync vaults directly between your own devices (see section 5).

Passphere helps you manage your credentials. It does not guarantee the security of the third-party accounts those credentials belong to.

4. Your Data and Master Password

You own your data. Everything you put in Passphere belongs to you. Your vault data stays on your devices, so we never receive it, store it or process it.

You are responsible for your master password. Because Passphere is zero-knowledge:

  • We cannot see, reset or recover your master password.
  • If you forget your master password, your vault cannot be decrypted by anyone, including us. This is by design.
  • You are responsible for keeping your master password and your unlocked devices secure.

Back up your vault. Your data lives only on your devices. It can be permanently lost if a device is lost, damaged or reset, the app is uninstalled, or browser or app storage is cleared. Please export an encrypted backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe. We cannot restore lost data.

5. Device Sync

Sync is optional and works peer-to-peer between your own devices. Quick Sync uses a third-party signalling service (PeerJS) only to set up a direct connection. Offline Sync works without any network. Data is encrypted before it leaves your device.

Sync depends on your devices, your network and third-party services that we do not control. We do not guarantee that sync will always be available or that it will never cause conflicts or data loss. Keep a backup before syncing.
